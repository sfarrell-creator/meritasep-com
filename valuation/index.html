Questions

Is this confidential?

Yes. We sign a mutual NDA before you send anything, we never contact your customers, employees or lenders, and nothing is shared outside Meritas.

What do I need to send?

Profit and loss statements and balance sheets for the last three years (tax returns are fine), year-to-date numbers, and revenue by your top ten customers. Customer names can be coded.

How accurate is the range?

It is the range we would expect serious buyers to discuss for your business today, based on normalized earnings and current market multiples. The final price in any deal depends on the buyer, terms and diligence, which is why we show you what moves it.

Who prepares it?

The partners of Meritas Equity Partners: a former Chief Revenue Officer who has worked in private equity, and a former Bank of America Managing Director. We invest in lower-middle-market companies in Upstate NY ourselves.

What happens after?

Nothing, unless you want it to. Some owners use the report to plan. Others start our three-week Value Diagnostic to build a roadmap for raising the number, and the $1,500 is credited in full.
